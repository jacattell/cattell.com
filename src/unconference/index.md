Unconferences are like conferences (but are different in 3 ways):

1. attendees create the agenda (not organisers),
2. it's about discussions (not presentations),
3. attendees face each other (not the stage).

Unconferences often:

    1. last a day or 2,
    2. start by creating an agenda,
    3. include parallel discussions that happen thoughout the event.

![Sticky notes containing discussion topics are stuck to a large glass wall, and arranged into rooms and times on a grid.](/assets/img/unconference-1.jpg)

The attendee sourced agenda from The Facilitator's Unconference 2024 in London, England.

I attend, organise and volunteer for unconferences big and small. I've done so for over a decade. Hence starting this post, book, whatever it becomes 🙂.

Initially, I used [National Novel Writing Month](https://nanowrimo.org/) (NaNoWriMo) in November 2023 to start this work. Special thanks to [Samantha Venia Logan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samanthavlogan/) for 1-2-1 and group writing sessions.

Thanks for reading this. I hope it’s useful. [Let me know](/contact/) whether or not.

I mostly learn by doing. My first unconference was [Local Gov Camp Yorkshire and Humber](https://localgov.digital/localgovcamp/2010-yorkshire-humber) on Saturday 12 June 2010. My latest was [The Facilitator's Unconference](https://www.facilitators-unconference.com/) on Wednesday 1 November 2023.

That’s over a decade of experience. Hence I call myself an unconference expert, and this book.

I am a senior [delivery manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/delivery-manager/). I have worked in central government since 2012.

A delivery manager makes sure a team delivers a product or service that works for the people who use it. I keep the team focused on what matters most, remove things that block them, and keep everyone informed.

## How I can help you

- **You are starting something new.** I can run a discovery or alpha to find out what users need before you spend money building it.
- **You have a live service.** I can help your team keep it running, improve it, and roll it out to more places.
- **Your team is new to agile.** I can coach your team on agile and lean ways of working, using your real work, not theory.
- **Your priorities have changed.** I can help your team change direction quickly without losing momentum.
- **You work with suppliers.** I can help you buy the right service, assure it, and manage the contract.

## How I work

- **Agile and lean.** I choose the method that suits the team and the work. I do not use a method just because it is popular.
- **Clear communication.** I explain technical work in plain English to managers and policy colleagues. I make sure the team is heard.
- **Planning.** I help the team agree priorities and plan work they can actually deliver.
- **Momentum.** I track risks, issues and dependencies. I deal with them early and escalate when I need to.
- **Simple processes.** I keep the processes that help and remove the ones that do not.
- **Through every phase.** I guide work from discovery to alpha, beta and live, and change my approach at each phase.
- **Money and contracts.** I manage budgets and balance cost against value. I manage relationships with suppliers.
- **Strong teams.** I build motivated teams, give them space to make decisions, and deal with problems openly.

Monday 15 to Friday 19 March 2021.

Where to start? New job!

[Jen](https://twitter.com/jenoliver_jen) and I completed our [Ministry of Justice](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-justice) (MoJ) pre-employment checks this week. Next we'll receive formal offer letters and transfer forms to move us from home departments.

Permanently.

My 'home' is the [Department for Digital, Culture, Media & Sport](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-digital-culture-media-sport) (DCMS) where I've never worked. Yes, the machinery of government (MoG) can be a fascinating and bewildering thing.

Years ago when I was on loan to the [Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-environment-food-rural-affairs) (Defra), my 'home' team in the [Cabinet Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/cabinet-office) got MoG'ged to DCMS. I didn't really think much about it at the time, because I was having loads of fun in Defra.

I actually consider my 'home' to be the Cabinet Office. This will stop next month and I'm not sure how I feel about it. On reflection I’m not sure it is my ‘home’ anymore. The [Civil Service](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/civil-service) is my ‘home’ (note to self: you can get rid of ‘quotes’ now).

I’ve always felt there’s a certain panache working in the Cabinet Office. I started there in 2012 as a [GOV.UK support](https://www.gov.uk/contact/govuk) lead in the [Government Digital Service](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-digital-service). I stayed on to do a [community developer manager](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/community-development-handbook/community-development-handbook#what-a-cdm-or-cmgr-is) (CDM) role, then moved to Cabinet Office ‘proper’ (quotes!) in the [transparency team](https://twitter.com/uktransparency).

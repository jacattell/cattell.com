Monday 11 to Sunday 17 October 2021.

I'm sitting at home on Sunday evening after a lovely, relaxing bath. Perusing Twitter I see [Sam Villis tweet her first week notes in a while](https://twitter.com/stamanfar/status/1449830625092018182). So, inspired, I start this.

- was inspired by Onyeka to put the word hacker back in my Twitter profile
- spent time with [Jen](https://twitter.com/jenoliver_jen) going over our work objectives
- we also offered to become co-chairs of the Ministry of Justice job sharing network
- did a retro with the team on our first local content managers community meetup
- joined my first [One Team Gov arround](https://medium.com/oneteamgov/one-team-gov-global-were-going-around-the-world-bf8965d7939d) the world meetup

- spent the rest of the weekend recoverying.

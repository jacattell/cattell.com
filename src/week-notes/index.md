## About week notes

The world wide web (or **web** for short) is a good place for you to keep a **log** of you're activities. It's lets people see who you are, what you do and why you're doing it. If you're so inclined, you also invite comments on what you share.

What you post to your web log (or **blog** for short) is entirely up to you. The things you post on your blog (a.k.a. **blog posts)** can take any format you like. A popular one is to post a note of what happened in your week. These **week notes** have many styles, some of which you can see on [Sam Villis](https://twitter.com/stamanfar)'s blog post on [Week note styles](https://weeknot.es/weeknote-styles-61d957e58a4a).

### My week notes journey

I want to stabilise my on / off relationship with week notes. I range from blogging every weekday to going months without a single post. Rather than setting another new year's resolution, I've made it my intention to publish a week note every week.

I was a member of the [weeknot.es community](https://weeknot.es) which is hosted on [Medium.com](https://medium.com/about) (a popular blogging platform). I sometimes see other members posts popup in my Twitter feed. I draw inspiration and encouragement from this community to help me blog better.

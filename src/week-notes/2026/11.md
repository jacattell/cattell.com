## Cheadle Community Library

[Lead trustee Ivan Wozniak](https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/en/charity-search/-/charity-details/5233103/full-print) asked me for updates as follows:

### Group email

Emails to [trustees@cheadlecommunitylibrary.org.uk](mailto:trustees@cheadlecommunitylibrary.org.uk) are sent to all library trustee's private email addresses. They can they reply to the sender from the group email address (not their private one), but this hasn't been successfully tested yet. This service uses [Google Groups](https://support.google.com/groups/answer/46601?hl=en).

This does not affect Jenni's management to the volunteer's rota, which uses a completely separate email address.

### Trustee calendar

Most trustees use paper calendars, so this shared calendar has limited use. Anyone can [view the calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=c_44096148d51e0eb066b61b59940bc0995af3eccdafb98bbf0da615a0168e6279%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=Europe%2FLondon), though its currently empty. Trustees can be invited to new events by adding [trustees@cheadlecommunitylibrary.org.uk](mailto:trustees@cheadlecommunitylibrary.org.uk) to calendar invites, but this hasn't been successfully tested yet.

### Wi-Fi

Volunteers and visitor can use 2 available wireless networks:

- Your Library (3 megabits per second, i.e. very slow)
- GovWifi (80 megabits per second, i.e. very fast)

Your Library requires a library account and internet access enabled.  
  
[GovWifi](https://www.wifi.service.gov.uk/) has no requirements, and can be joined by texting "Go" to 07537 417 417. Initial setup can take a few minutes, but only has to be done once per device. This has been successfully tested on 5 devices.

## Deliver Con 2024.2

[Deliver Con](https://delivercon.uk/) is an [unconference](/unconference/) for [delivery managers](https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/role/delivery-manager) in and around the public sector. It’s been run in person annually since 2018, with the exception of 2019 because of [COVID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).

In 2024 it became biannual, i.e. run twice a year. First in Cardiff on 18 April and second in London on 7 November. The latter was organized by [Samuel Hoskin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samuel-hoskin/), [Elodie Farrar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elodie-farrar-a4499a90/) and I.

Unlike conferences where the agenda is set in advance, unconference agendas are set on the day. And the agenda’s full of discussions, not presentations.

To source the agenda, I used the [David Heath](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davidheath/) / [Liberating Structures](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/) method. It combines [Impromptu Networking](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/2-impromptu-networking/) and [25/10 Crowdsourcing](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/12-2510-crowd-sourcing/).

The 25/10 part requires a card and pen. On the front you write your discussion topic. On the back it gets scored. To simplify the scoring, we stamped the back with rows of numbers that people could circle.

![2 pieces of card and a pen are laid out on a wooden table. 1 piece of card is blank and is labelled "front". The other has rows of numbers on it and is labelled "back".](/assets/img/week-notes-2024-45-1.jpg)

Things needed for 25/10 crowdsourcing

Samuel sourced the cards, [rubber stamp](https://amzn.eu/d/jbaQK7g) and ink pad. Then we sat with Elodie and stamped the cards.

## Ignorance as a superpower

1 of the first sessions at Deliver Con 2024.2 was, "Top tips for being a great delivery manager". I sat listening for a while whilst [updating the online agenda](https://github.com/Samuel-Hoskin/DeliverConSite).

Then I heard [Nic Macken](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nic-macken-5020651a4/) talk about using simple questions as a way to create safe spaces. I've been using this technique and thinking about it for a while. So I wondered over and joined the discussion circle.

After listening for a while, I put my hand up and eventually said, "As delivery managers we use ignorance as a superpower to create safe spaces".

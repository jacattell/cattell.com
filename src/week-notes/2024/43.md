## TCC Happy Hour

[The Community Community](https://thecommunitycommunity.com/) (TCC) is a community for senior community managers. I recently joined TCC’s leadership team as events lead. I got the job in part because of my ongoing hosting of Happy Hour, a weekly hour long chat on [Slack](https://slack.com/).

TCC’s amazing founder [Nikki Thibodeau](https://communitynikki.com/) runs Tuesday’s chat, and I run Thursday’s. We have 2 to cater for different time zones, with Thursday’s being 3pm Central European Time.

The format’s usually:

- a warm up exercise / question,
- 3 other questions on any theme,
- thank attendees and ask for feedback.

There’s a [list of questions](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16HCMTmmnHXqMYIEJiuaaxf9U9hQtQPhZX_vD4E7Z5Bg/edit?usp=sharing) we’ve used before, so you can get an idea of the flow. But this week I doodled my prep / thought process and thought I’d share it with you.

![A piece of A4 paper full of handwritten notes in various colours.](/assets/img/week-notes-2024-43-1.jpg)

I like to start with something really simple, to get people going. By simple I mean you don’t really need to think about the answer, you just share what’s happening. So this week it was:

- How are you feeling today?
- \[share an optional picture\]

Then I try to slowly increase the mental effort to answer the question.

So this week’s first proper question was:

- Where or when do you feel most at peace?

Then I tried:

- What do you really, really care about?

And finally:

- What’s your greatest hope?

I finish off by thanking everyone for coming and [seeking feedback](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScKto9tY-KLVLaOWdLPGIPiVlca8h3swfEbqq_2SvCttxS2_w/viewform?usp=sf_link). Once that last message is sent, I reply to it in-thread and personally mention / thank people who contributed.

Anyway, just thought I’d share it, as it’s something I love doing 🙂

Please do [apply to join TCC](https://thecommunitycommunity.com/pages/the-community-community-application-form) if you’re interested.

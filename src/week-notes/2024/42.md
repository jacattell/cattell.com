## OggCamp 2024

I was at the [Manchester Conference Centre](https://www.pendulumhotel.co.uk/) on Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 October to volunteer at [OggCamp](https://oggcamp.org/). This 2 day event has an open source theme, with 2 speaker tracks and 2 unconference tracks. My job was to manage the [unconference schedule](https://joind.in/event/oggcamp-2024).

Although an unconference can be whatever you like, most of the unconference sessions were actually talks. Apparently there were some discussions, but they were in the minority. But that’s okay 🙂

I enjoyed getting to know a new crowd of people, and really getting my geek on for the first time in ages.

## ukgc25

UKGovCamp 2025 (ukgc25) is an annual event for people interested in making the public sector better. I’m the event’s delivery manager and this week we had our 2nd regular organiser’s catchup (4th meeting in total).

[Registration is open and venue search is underway.](https://www.ukgovcamp.com/2024/10/08/ukgc25-registration-venue-search/)

Just have a few more organiser roles to fill, then we’ll be up to full strength. Several of us regular organisers are standing down this year, so it’s good to welcome new blood and help them fly.

Personally, I’m moving into the treasurer role that’s been done by [Steph Gray](https://stephgray.com/) for many years. He doesn’t live far from me, so I cycled over and started the handover today.

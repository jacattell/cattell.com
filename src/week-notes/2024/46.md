## Juggling stuff

In [last week’s uncompleted note](/week-notes/2024/45/), I had an empty section titled, "Something has to give...".

I now realise I was coming out of the tail end of some depression / undiagnosed seasonal affective disorder. It’s been 2 weeks since [the clocks changed](https://www.gov.uk/when-do-the-clocks-change), which tends to hit me quite hard at this time of year.

But the big reason is Janet’s been away all month, due to [a family bereavement](https://x.com/JanetHughes/status/1853237711257100594). Not having her next to me when I’m sleeping is challenging - I miss her. Last night for example I tried a hot bath, applying all my [Protopic ointment](https://bnf.nice.org.uk/drugs/tacrolimus/) and having the bedroom windows open (I prefer a cold sleeping environment). But I was still tossing and turning at 4am 😞 That typed, I’ve got quite a bit done today. Plus I’ve been taking my very limited supply of ADHD meds all week (see below).

Upside of last night’s temporary insomnia, is that I went through my [public Trello board](https://trello.com/b/ok6YJCH7) and trimmed it, which made me feel better. ~~List~~ trim all the things!

## ADHD meds

I currently take 72mg of prolonged release [Methylphenidate](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/methylphenidate-adults/). I take the whole dose in the morning when I wake up and it helps me focus throughout the day. It does a pretty good job and has certainly helped me this week.

But:

- I will run out next week,
- my nominated pharmacy can’t get hold of any more,
- Boots’s [prescription stock checker](https://www.boots.com/online/psc/) is also empty (and I’ve tried various places around the country.

![List of locations from the Boots stock checker, all of show my drugs are out of stock.](/assets/img/week-notes-2024-46-1.jpg)

Computer says no.

There are reports of supply shortages on various government and NHS websites, but nothing about a solution. So I reckon I’m gonna have to change medication, probably to [Lisdexamfetamine Mesilate](https://bnf.nice.org.uk/drugs/lisdexamfetamine-mesilate/). Will message my Doctor’s tomorrow. Fingers crossed 🤞🏻

## Funemployed

It’s 2 weeks since I stopped working. Still getting used to it. Feels wrong.

Workwise, I do this to find new:

- connections,
- things I enjoy,
- sources of income.

Personally, I do this to:

- decompress,
- reflect,
- stop.

By stop, I mean:

- sit still,
- just breath,
- do nothing else.

## More busy than ever(ish)

I fill my time with stuff, rather than do the stuff above. I distract myself. So I’ve blocked time on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings to cycle, sit and reflect. That’s all I’ll do.

## New co-working space

On my 2nd day of funemployment, I went to a [meetup](https://lu.ma/london-meetup-april). It was held at [Fabrik](https://joinfabrik.com/), a co-working space for community builders. It’s in Old Street, London.

The atmosphere and people are nice. So I joined and became member number 42. I can bring a guest everyday, so [contact me](/contact/) if that’s you.

Saturday 8 to Friday 14 January 2022.

It was my first week back at work after a lovely, long, quiet break. Was slightly complicated by the fact I’d been isolating since the previous Wednesday. Was completely asymptomatic, thankfully. Following new guidance, I tested negative 2 days in a row and got out of the house on Thursday. Huzzah!

Had my second session with a new private therapist this week. Really went all in and talked about the worst psychotic experience I’ve been and what I think caused it. This had a bit of a knock on effect for the rest of the day, so might have to slow down a bit and create a better barrier. But otherwise I’m happy with the choice of therapist so far.

Been brilliant to be back at work with Jen and cracking on with stuff. Our product owner’s away on leave this week, so we’ve been building up a list of things to discuss with them on their return.

Had our regular catch-up with our service owner and had a good chat about things and asked loads of questions.

Jane in our team’s now had their [2nd blog post published](https://mojdigital.blog.gov.uk/2022/01/11/how-hmpps-cross-agency-collaboration-is-set-to-help-reduce-re-offending-rates/) and has plenty more in the pipeline.

Okay, I do have more to type, but the curry’s been delivered. Will be back later. Maybe tomorrow.

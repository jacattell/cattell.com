Saturday 24 to Sunday 30 January 2022.

Well, things haven't quite gone to plan over the last few weeks. But that's okay, plans change. Expect change.

Friday I couldn't focus. Was having real problems in handover with [Jen](https://twitter.com/jenoliver_jen). Several times had to ask for clarification. The afternoon didn't get any better and I decided to jack it in and try again on Monday. Will work a full day, rather than half day.

The [Ministry of Justice](https://mojdigital.blog.gov.uk/) (MoJ) had me on part 1 of [Having Difficult Conversations](https://www.quanta.co.uk/courses/management-development/having-difficult-conversations) (HDC) course this week. It gave me a lot to think about, especially how my emotions can alter HDC. Really interesting. Looking forward to part 2.

Therapy was okay this week. I came at it with an idea, well a question, which we talked thru and decided on a course of action.

Had a good Deliver Con 2022 organisers meet-up. Tickets are now available, and I need to email them to previous year's attendees.

[Bethany](https://twitter.com/BethMindham), [Emma](https://twitter.com/Emma_delivers) and I had our first Delivery Manager's Podcast meet-up. We even recorded a [little something](https://soundcloud.com/deliverconuk-information/delivery-manager-dm-podcast-discovery-with-beth-emma-an-james) and put it out there. Hopefully [Greg](https://twitter.com/gh0lt) will join us on the next one. Really looking forward to making something of this. Coincidentally, I found [The Delivery Manager Daily](https://twitter.com/dm_daily).

[Imran](https://twitter.com/ImHuYorks) and I have finished our first piece of private community work. I really love working with him cause he keeps me honest, inspires me and helps me see the world in a better light.

It was Community Manager Appreciation Day 2022 ([#CMAD2022](https://twitter.com/hashtag/CMAD2022)) this week. [CMX tweeted a 75% discount](https://twitter.com/CMX/status/1485674085698912257) on their courses, so I reinvested my earnings in their [CMX Academy](https://cmxhub.com/academy/) courses. I've also noticed the [Community Roundtable](https://communityroundtable.com/what-we-do/thecr-network-resources-for-community-managers/) have membership but it's £1,500. That'll have to wait.

Other stuff has happened, but I don't wanna overdo it.

Toodle pip.

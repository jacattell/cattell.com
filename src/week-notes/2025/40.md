## We have a date!

Completed my 2ᶮᵈ week at [The Pension Regulator](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/the-pensions-regulator) (TPR) 🥳 I've now heard back from all members of the new [Digital, Data and Technology (DDaT) working group](https://blog.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/2025/07/01/how-collaboration-will-help-pensions-thrive-in-the-digital-data-and-technology-age/). This means we have a preferred date for the group's 1ˢᵗ meeting in November. Its not unanimous, but we need to crack on.

## Work in public (service)

Had some lovely feedback from 1 of the DDaT working group members. They liked my [public kanban board](https://trello.com/b/aqrsaRJa) and [week notes](/week-notes/2025/39/). Will share the feedback internally.

Also very grateful to [Lisa Allen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lisa-allen-data111/) for feedback on [week 39's note](/week-notes/2025/39/). I share my note here, on socials and internally at TPR, so its important to use the right language. This is especially true about the pensions industry, which I have a lot to learn about.

Also thankful for my other half, the awesome [Janet Hughes](https://www.linkedin.com/in/janet-hughes/). She shared the "donkey, sheep, fox, owl" model for thinking about language and its impact.

Janet suggested I'm sometimes too much of a sheep, i.e. full of integrity but lacking in political awareness. An example is talking publicly about "Open Pensions" without 1ˢᵗ discussing the concept internally with TPR comms teams.

The [Civil Service code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/civil-service-code/the-civil-service-code) guides us to be honest, objective, impartial and do everything with integrity. I once had my integrity called into question for [tweeting about Microsoft](https://x.com/search?q=surface%20rt%20%28from%3Ajacattell%29%20until%3A2013-08-01%20since%3A2012-11-01&src=typed_query&f=top) too much (even though I was being critical). So its probable I've over compensated for a while.

What I actually need to be is more owl, i.e. full of integrity and political awareness. This means seeking more permission and less forgiveness. I can easily achieve this by getting a 2ᶮᵈ pair of eyes to check anything new or that gives me pause.

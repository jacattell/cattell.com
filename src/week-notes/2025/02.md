## 2025 ~~goals~~ habits

I use the new year to set goals. But not this year. Instead I will focus [2025](/week-notes/2025/) on how I achieve goals.

I will establish, improve (or stop) habits that get stuff done (or not). More on this as the year progresses.

FYI, this was inspired by a [Next Big Idea Podcast](https://nextbigideaclub.com/podcast/) episode which features an interview with [James Clear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Clear) who wrote [Atomic Habits](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits-summary).

## Reflections on 2024

My [goal for 2024](/week-notes/2024/) was to come to terms with my imposter syndrome. I made a lot of progress with this, to the point where I now instantly recognise when it happens.

I stopped working in the public sector in April 2024. Then hung out at a [co-working space](https://www.joinfabrik.com) and picked up gigs back in the private sector. Turns out I’m not ready to be a freelancer, but might try again in a few years.

I helped setup and run the [Community-led World Summit London 2024](https://community.tightknit.ai/events/cevt_WtagPwtzrdjj/the-community-led-world-summit-london--online) doing:

- speaker coordination,
- stage management,
- technical support.

Delivered lots more unconferences including:

- [The Community Community](https://thecommunitycommunity.com/)’s 2nd unconference,
- LedBy Community’s 1st [Unconference Plus](https://newsletter.ledby.community/p/week-103),
- 2nd Facilitator’s Unconference,
- 2 x DeliverCons in [Cardiff](https://crossgovdelivery.uk/events/DeliverCon2024/) and [London](https://crossgovdelivery.uk/events/DeliverCon2024.2),
- Prepped the next [UKGovCamp](https://www.ukgovcamp.com) (see below)

I got paid to do 2 of them, so there’s still hope I can 1 day just delivery manage unconferences, and nothing else.

Also worked with [Conexen](https://conexen.net/) as event manager for a few months. But fell ill towards the end of the year and had to stop.

I’m better now and looking forward to my first unconference of 2025.

## [UKGovCamp](https://www.ukgovcamp.com)’s next week!

The biggest, annual, public sector unconference is back - and it’s next week on Saturday 18 January 2025.

I’m the lead organiser (AKA delivery manager) and it’s been more challenging than usual, mainly because we started planning late and I was unwell.

But this time next week (it’s 6:37pm as I type) my fellow organisers and I will be setting up the venue, then heading over to the social event nearby.

I really look forward to this every year.

Oooh, and I'm also now the treasurer.

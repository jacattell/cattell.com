## Who I am

I'm James Arthur Cattell. I run this website for myself. If you have a question about your information, [contact me](/contact/).

## No cookies or tracking

This website does not set cookies for visitors. Cloudflare, which hosts it, may set a cookie that helps block attacks. The website does not use advertising or tracking code. It does not load anything from other websites, except where this page says so.

## Visitor numbers

[Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) counts how many people visit each page. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

## Contact and feedback forms

When you send a form, what you write is emailed to me through [Resend](https://resend.com). It is not stored on this website. I keep emails only as long as I need them.

The forms may show Cloudflare's "Are you human?" check ([Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/application-services/products/turnstile/)) to stop spam.

## Ask my AI

When you use the [Ask my AI](/chat/) page:

- your questions are sent to [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), which makes the AI, to get an answer
- your questions and the answers are kept for 30 days, so I can see how the page is used, then deleted automatically
- your IP address is not kept with them

Do not share personal information on that page.

## Hosting

This website is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Like all web hosts, Cloudflare handles your IP address to send you pages and to block attacks.

## Your rights

You can ask me what information I have about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
